Build a Premium Personal Finance & Multi-Wallet App
Build a complete, production-quality personal finance management application called MALI – مالي.
The app is a personal money tracker, digital wallet manager, and accounting dashboard. It allows users to manage multiple cash wallets and bank accounts, track income and expenses, transfer money between accounts, monitor balances, and analyze their financial activity.
The app must be fully functional, not just a visual prototype.
1. Design direction
Create a premium fintech interface inspired by modern banking and financial apps such as Revolut, Wise, and Monzo, but with its own unique visual identity.
Style
- Premium, minimalist, elegant.
- Dark theme by default.
- Deep charcoal background: "#101318"
- Card background: "#1B2026"
- Primary accent: emerald green "#19C37D"
- Secondary accent: muted gold "#D6B56D"
- Text: white and soft gray.
- Rounded cards, subtle borders, elegant shadows.
- Smooth micro-interactions.
- High-quality charts and typography.
- No clutter, no unnecessary gradients, no excessive decorations.
Arabic support
- Arabic as the primary language.
- Full RTL support.
- Correct Arabic font rendering.
- Numbers and financial values must be readable.
- Support French and English in the future.
- Mobile-first design.
